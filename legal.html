Application Overview

Welcome to Money Matters. Our app helps users aggregate and track personal asset valuations, recurring transactions, net worth goals, and household finances seamlessly.

Google OAuth & Drive Integration

Money Matters connects to your Google Account to allow storing, importing, and exporting your financial data directly within your own personal Google Drive spreadsheets.

  • Least Privilege Permission: We strictly request access to the drive.file scope. This means our application can only read, edit, or create files that you explicitly create with or open through our application.
  • Data Ownership: Your financial data remains entirely inside your own Google Drive spreadsheet under your full ownership.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you log in using Google Authentication, we collect basic profile information necessary for session management:

  • Your primary email address
  • Basic profile details (Name, Profile Picture)

2. Google Drive Permissions (`drive.file`)

Our application requests the limited https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This scope is used solely to create and manage the specific Google Sheets files created by or explicitly opened with Money Matters.

We do NOT have access to any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to render your personal finance dashboard, compute balance sheet trends, and manage recurring budget targets. We do not sell, license, or share your personal financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your spreadsheet data lives directly within your Google Drive account, you retain 100% control over your data. You may revoke our access at any time via your Google Account Permissions page or delete the created spreadsheet directly from your Google Drive.
