I was recently reading through Y Combinator’s latest Request for Startups (RFS), and one theme stood out to me: AI-native. It sounds gentle but it is a disruptive shift. For the last decade, we’ve been "adding on" cloud and AI to existing structures. But as history suggests, the real winners aren't the ones who integrate; they’re the ones who are born in the new medium.
