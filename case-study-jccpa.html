The Challenge: Data-Rich, Insight-Poor

Managing 147 devices across multiple floors creates a massive stream of data. Without AI, this requires constant "dashboard watching," which takes staff away from resident care. I applied Strategic Design to filter 92 million annual data points into a handful of critical, actionable insights.

AI Capability: Anomaly Detection

The AI learns "normal" patterns (e.g., CO2 spikes at noon in the dining area). If levels spike at 3 AM or temperature drops rapidly (indicating a window left open), the system sends immediate Telegram alerts.

AI Capability: Wellness Reporting

Automated weekly reports compare melanopic light against time of day. Suggestion: "Room 1F-Living had insufficient emel (150) during peak hours; increase brightness to 80%."
