The challenge: too much data, not enough useful information

The centre’s 147 devices across several floors generate around 92 million data points each year. Reviewing them manually would take staff away from resident care. I helped design a system that highlights unusual patterns and brings important information to staff’s attention.

Spot unusual changes

The system learns typical conditions for each area—for example, higher CO₂ around lunchtime in the dining room. If readings change unexpectedly, such as a CO₂ spike overnight or a sudden drop in temperature, it can send staff an alert.

Make lighting easier to assess

Weekly reports show whether lighting levels match the time of day and suggest where adjustments may help.

Equipment developed for the project

CL-ROUND-500

CircadianLux

Designed to support the body’s natural 24-hour clock, this Bluetooth-enabled light adjusts brightness and colour temperature based on location.

• CRI 95+ for accurate colour rendering

• Adjustable brightness from 1% to 100%

• UV-free for use in care environments

RY1 SENSOR

RoomYou Wellbeing Sensor

This sensor measures light in a way that reflects its effect on the body’s daily rhythm. It records room conditions every five minutes.

• CO2 Range: 0 to 9999 ppm

• Accuracy: ±0.2°C Temperature Precision

• E-ink display that is easy to read and uses little power
